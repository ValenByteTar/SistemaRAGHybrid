ISO/IEC 27001 — Information Security Management System

ISO/IEC 27001 is the leading international standard for information security
management systems (ISMS). It provides a systematic approach to managing sensitive
company information so that it remains secure. The standard was originally published
in 2005 and has been updated several times, with the most recent version being
ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

The standard defines 93 controls (reduced from 114 in the 2013 version) organized
into 4 themes:

1. Organizational controls (37 controls) — Cover policies, roles, responsibilities,
   asset management, supplier relationships, and compliance.

2. People controls (8 controls) — Address screening, terms of employment, awareness
   training, disciplinary process, and remote working.

3. Physical controls (14 controls) — Include secure areas, equipment security,
   clear desk and clear screen policies, and physical entry controls.

4. Technological controls (34 controls) — Encompass access control, cryptography,
   secure development, system acquisition, and network security.

The 93 controls are grouped into domains such as:
- Information security policies (5.1-5.36)
- Human resource security (6.1-6.6)
- Asset management (7.1-7.14)
- Access control (8.1-8.28)
- Cryptography (8.24)
- Physical and environmental security (7.1-7.14)
- Operations security (8.1-8.28)
- Communications security (8.1-8.28)
- System acquisition, development and maintenance (8.1-8.28)
- Supplier relationships (15.1-15.6)
- Information security incident management (16.1-16.2)
- Information security aspects of business continuity management (17.1-17.2)
- Compliance (18.1-18.2)

The ISMS implementation follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle:
- PLAN: Define the scope, policy, and risk assessment methodology.
- DO: Implement the risk treatment plan, controls, and policies.
- CHECK: Monitor and measure the ISMS performance against the policy and objectives.
- ACT: Take corrective and preventive actions based on the results of the ISMS audit
  and management review.

Certification to ISO 27001 is performed by accredited certification bodies and
involves a two-stage audit process. Stage 1 is a documentation review, and Stage 2
is a full compliance audit.
